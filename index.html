Add to Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the icon, not from a browser tab — iOS is far less likely to discard a Home Screen app, and saved work survives.

Working offline

  1. Open this page once with signal. All three tools cache automatically.
  2. After that they open with no signal, at the dock or offshore.
  3. Entries are held on the phone until you export. Do not clear Safari data mid-survey.
